# **App Name**: ChicoryConnect

## Core Features:

- Multi-Language Support: Implement a multi-language selector in the header to support global accessibility and scalability.
- B2B Contact Channels: Display WhatsApp icon on every page for instant B2B communication; include prominent contact forms on product and contact pages.
- Detailed Product Pages: Develop comprehensive product pages detailing specs, applications, MOQ, and pricing, crucial for B2B procurement.
- Informative Blog: Create a professional blog to publish industry-relevant content, enhancing SEO and establishing thought leadership.
- AI Blog Topic Generation: Generate industry-relevant blog post topics using generative AI tool to attract B2B audience.
- Streamlined Navigation: Design clear navigation that prioritizes B2B needs, focusing on product discovery, contact options, and company information.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Olive green (#808000), representing the natural aspect of chicory and its earthy origins.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC), providing a neutral and clean backdrop that complements the primary color.
- Accent color: Golden brown (#A52A2A), used for CTAs and highlights to draw attention to important elements and reinforce the B2B brand aesthetic.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif, for a clean and modern professional look.
- FontAwesome icons used, B2B and industry specific to convey industry competence
- Incorporate a clear, scalable layout suitable for presenting product specs and B2B information in a user-friendly manner.
- Subtle loading animations to maintain a polished, professional feel during user interactions.
